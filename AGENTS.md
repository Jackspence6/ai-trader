# Session protocol (Meridian)

At the start of every working session, read `OPPORTUNITIES.md` — it is the
living ledger of what has shipped, what is in flight, what is ranked next,
and which income avenues already have dated verdicts. Choose work from its
NEXT UP list unless the operator directs otherwise. At the end of the
session, update it: move shipped work, re-rank, add new ideas with dated
verdicts. Never re-argue a REJECTED verdict without new evidence.
